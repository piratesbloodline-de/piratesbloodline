Artist

Pirates Bloodline is a fusion of cultures and hypnotic grooves, a boundary-breaking DJ and sonic storyteller with an instinct for rhythm and a deep connection to cultural roots. His sets draw from the far reaches of the globe, blending mesmerizing beats, pulsing basslines, and hypnotic melodies into a rich, ever evolving soundscape.


Each performance becomes a vessel, carrying ancestral echoes and tales through tides, the rebellious spirit of dance itself. He doesn't play genres. He builds atmospheres. 

"The dancefloor becomes a shared voyage, where beats blur reality and the power of sound unites all"
— Available for quote from venue / promoter

Over fifteen years, his sound moved through continents and contexts, from the clubs of Dubai & Istanbul to the underground floors of Berlin, Copenhagen, Paris and Zurich. Heavy percussion, vocal chants in languages you can't place, edits that land before you see them coming.

Circie started in 2019 as Kashay Salon, a cultural space that evolved, deepened, and became Circie: nightclub, record label and a booking platform connecting artists across Europe. 

Let's sail.

Technical Rider
RIDER
Technical & Hospitality · 2026
DJ Setup
  • 2× Pioneer CDJ 2000 NXS2 / CDJ 3000 / CDJ 3000x linked (3 decks always welcome)
  • 1× Pioneer DJM-900 NXS2 or  DJM-A9 or DJM-V10 or equivalent professional mixer
  • 2× Monitor speakers with independent volume control, pointed toward artist
Sound & Production
  • High-quality PA appropriate for venue capacity
  • Professional lighting system
  • Sound technician available throughout
  • Lighting technician available throughout
Hospitality
  • Hotel or private room
  • Late checkout - no earlier than 12:00, ideally 13:00/14:00
  • One meal on the day of performance
Drinks at the Gig
  • Still water (on stage, always)
  • Sparkling water
  • Soft drinks
  • Light snacks (fruit, nuts, dates)
  • Any additional hospitality is warmly welcomed and left to the venue's discretion.

Note: For deviations from this rider, contact us in advance - we will always find a solution. No recording without prior written permission from the artist or Circie.
